Our Story

Rooted in Kirimaini, Growing With Our Farmers

Kirimaini Goat Farm sits on the fertile slopes of Ol Kalou in Nyandarua County — a region famous for cool air, clean water and lush green pasture. That environment shapes every animal we raise.

From a Small Herd to a Trusted Name

We started with just a handful of local does and one borrowed he-goat. What we lacked in numbers, we made up for in patience and discipline. We studied breeding cycles, invested in quality feed, and refused to cut corners on animal health.

Today, farmers travel from across Nyandarua, Laikipia, Nakuru and Nyeri to buy from us. Not because we are the biggest farm — but because our goats do what they are supposed to do: grow fast, breed reliably and stay healthy.

Every sale carries our name. That is why we treat every animal as if it were staying with us forever.

Our Livestock

Breeds & Types Available

We keep a mixed herd so that every buyer — whether you want meat, milk, breeding stock or a hardy grazer — finds exactly the right animal.

🐐

Boer & Boer Crosses

Kenya's leading meat breed. Exceptional growth rate, heavy muscling and excellent feed conversion. Ideal for commercial meat production and crossbreeding with local does.

🐏

Galla (Somali) Goats

Hardy, drought-tolerant and famous for high milk yield. Perfect for arid and semi-arid areas where other breeds struggle to survive.

🐑

Small East African

Native, resilient and low-maintenance. Excellent mothers, disease-resistant, and the smartest choice for first-time keepers on a tight budget.

🐐

Toggenburg & Alpine

Dairy specialists producing 2–3 litres of rich milk daily. Calm temperament, easy to milk and highly adaptable to highland conditions.

🐏

Kalahari Red

Beautiful red-coated meat breed with outstanding heat tolerance and a strong carcass. A favourite among farmers targeting premium butcher markets.

🐑

Crossbreed He-Goats

Powerful, proven sires that pass on fast growth and strong conformation to your herd. The fastest way to upgrade your entire flock's genetics.

Knowledge & Benefits

Why Goats Are the Smartest Investment in Kenya

Goats are often called "the poor man's cow" — but that name undersells them badly. They are fast, efficient, low-risk and remarkably profitable when handled correctly.

🍖

Health Benefits of Goat Meat

Goat meat (chevon) is one of the healthiest red meats available:

  • Lower in saturated fat than beef, lamb or pork
  • Higher in iron and protein per gram
  • Easier to digest — gentle on the stomach
  • Rich in vitamin B12, zinc and potassium
  • Recommended for low-cholesterol diets
🥛

Goat Milk — Nature's Complete Food

Goat milk is far more than a by-product:

  • Smaller fat globules — easier to digest than cow milk
  • Naturally A2 protein, gentler for sensitive stomachs
  • High in calcium, phosphorus and vitamin A
  • Excellent for children, the elderly and convalescents
  • Strong demand in health-conscious urban markets
💵

Financial Benefits for Farmers

A goat is one of the fastest-maturing livestock investments in East Africa:

  • Does can produce 2–3 kids per year
  • Reach market weight in 12–18 months
  • Very low feed cost — they browse almost anything
  • Small starting capital compared to cattle
  • Steady demand during festive seasons and ceremonies
🌱

Practical Farm Advantages

Goats fit into almost any farm setup:

  • Need very little space — a small shed is enough
  • Thrive on shrubs, weeds and crop residues
  • Manure is premium organic fertiliser
  • Hardy and adaptable to different climates
  • Easy to handle, even for women and children

Turn Grass Into Money

The farmers who succeed with goats are the ones who start with strong, healthy stock. Give your investment the best possible beginning.
